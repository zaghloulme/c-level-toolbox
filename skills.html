Two skills to start: setup collects your preferences and writes user-config.md; update-profile changes any preference later. Every other skill reads that config to personalize output.

Every skill lives in skills/<name>/SKILL.md. Frontmatter defines what the skill does; the body defines how Claude produces the deliverable.
